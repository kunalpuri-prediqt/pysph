## Problems encountered

- Vue's runtime template compiler discards `<style>` tags, so `html.Style(...)`
  had been silently dropped since the app was built; the old look came entirely
  from inline element styles.
- `SinglePageWithDrawerLayout` keeps a flex-grow toolbar title slot ahead of
  appended children, which centred the brand lockup.
- The offscreen render window kept its construction size, letterboxing the
  streamed frame; `client.SizeObserver` emits
  `{"size": {...}, "pixelRatio": ...}` rather than a flat rect, so the first
  handler silently no-opped.
