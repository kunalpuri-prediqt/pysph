# Session - 2026-08-04 14:39

## Goal of this session

Review whether the current Warp dam-break studio combines adaptive particle
resolution with a floating obstacle, then execute the approved ten-item
production-track plan as far as its scientific gates permit.

## Findings

- The studio is a two-level dynamically adaptive **fluid** smoke demo: coarse
  fluid particles split inside a fixed geometry box and complete fine families
  merge outside it at explicit checkpoints.
- The obstacle in this runner is fixed. `WarpDamBreakSimulation.step()` passes
  both wall and obstacle arrays to `wc_sph_dam_break_step`, whose solid arrays
  receive density/pressure updates but zero acceleration and therefore remain
  stationary.
- A separate uniform-grid floating-box path exists through
  `wc_sph_dam_break_rigid_step` and `warp_floating_box_runner.py`. It has
  device-resident 6-DOF state and Liu fluid/rigid coupling, but is not exposed
  by `WarpDamBreakSimulation`, its worker, or the studio UI.
- The missing target combination is adaptive fluid plus the floating rigid
  body. The rigid step currently hard-codes `neighbor_mode='grid'`, whereas the
  adaptive runner uses `MultilevelGridWarpNNPS` and `neighbor_mode='multilevel'`.
- Production APR work remains: a literature-reconciled transfer operator,
  transition correction/shifting, device-side particle allocation/compaction,
  longer merge/impact/convergence evidence, and uniform-fine correctness and
  performance gates.
- Floating-body P4 remains: rigid-wall contact, longer-case stability/EPEC
  guard, tier-2 CPU parity, and the assembled surge-tosses-a-box case.
