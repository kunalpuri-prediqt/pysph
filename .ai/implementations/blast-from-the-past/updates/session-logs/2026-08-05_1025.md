  new asymmetric variable-`h` Liu oracle conserves net force and torque.

  averaged-`HIJ` correction worsens interface horizontal residual RMS by
  12.74% and vertical hydrostatic residual RMS by 26.75%.
- Floating-equilibrium screens improve vertically and rotationally from
  `dx=0.1` to `dx=1/12`, but retain sign-changing lateral drift. Uniform
  coarse-to-fine angular velocity differs 86.76%, so torque is not converged
  even without adaptation.
